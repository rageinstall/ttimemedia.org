Be in it

T-Time runs on other people's stories, not just mine — and that only works if the range is real.

I'm looking for trans men and transmasculine people across race, age, body type, class, sexuality, and background, whether or not you've medically transitioned, whether or not you "pass." If you want to be featured — a portrait, an interview, a story in your own words — start here. Before anything is published, here is what you're agreeing to and what you keep.

  • Written permission first. Nothing runs without it.
  • You set the detail level. Full name, first name, or none.
  • You can withdraw. Before or after publication, no reason needed.
  • No location giveaways. Backgrounds get checked before posting.
  • You see it first. Nothing is published you haven't approved.
  • Adults only. T-Time does not feature minors.
