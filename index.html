What this is

T-Time Media is an independent media production company creating original and partner-driven work across culture, identity, lifestyle, and community.

Founded by Dominic Anthony Pérignon.

We produce original series, interviews, branded content, and social-first media — working across formats and platforms without losing sight of the people at the center of the story.

T-Time Series, our flagship, is about transgender men and transmasculine people — our lives, our history, and how we get covered. That includes men who've medically transitioned and men who haven't, men who "pass" and men who don't, binary and nonbinary transmasculine people alike — T-Time Series isn't defining who counts.

In the 2024–25 U.S. television season, 7 of 33 transgender characters were trans men. None were gay trans men. GLAAD, Where We Are on TV, 2024–25 season

Meet the creator

Dominic Anthony Pérignon

Founder, T-Time Media · Journalist · Storyteller

Dominic Anthony Pérignon is the founder and creative force behind T-Time Media, a production company built around culture, identity, lifestyle, and community. He's a journalist and storyteller who got his start covering the lived experiences of transgender men and transmasculine people — work that grew into T-Time Media's slate of original series and partner projects. His work is rooted in the belief that the people at the center of a story deserve to be represented not just as subjects of debate, but as people with histories, experiences, voices, creativity, and stories worth documenting.

"I started T-Time because trans men's stories were missing — from mainstream LGBTQ+ conversations, media coverage, even our own community spaces. I wanted to put us at the center: to document our history, amplify our voices, and create a place where trans men can finally see themselves reflected." — Dominic, on why he started T-Time Series
