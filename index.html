What this is

T-Time is a media series about transgender men and transmasculine people — our lives, our history, and how we get covered.

Made by Dominic Anthony Pérignon. Published on Instagram and Facebook.

That includes men who've medically transitioned and men who haven't, men who "pass" and men who don't, binary and nonbinary transmasculine people alike — T-Time isn't defining who counts.

Some of it is history. Some of it is reporting on how newsrooms and studios handle us. A lot of it is just men — at work, at home, in the middle of an ordinary Tuesday.

Every claim here is sourced and dated. When historians disagree about the details, T-Time names the disagreement instead of quietly picking whichever version reads better.

In the 2024–25 U.S. television season, 7 of 33 transgender characters were trans men. None were gay trans men. GLAAD, Where We Are on TV, 2024–25 season

Meet the creator

Dominic Anthony Pérignon

Independent Journalist · Content Creator · Founder, T-Time Media

Dominic Anthony Pérignon is a journalist, content creator, and storyteller focused on LGBTQ+ communities, with a particular focus on the lived experiences of transgender men. Outside of T-Time Media, he creates educational and personal content, builds community, and uses his platform to make complex issues more visible and understandable. His work is rooted in the belief that trans people deserve to see themselves represented not just as subjects of debate, but as people with histories, experiences, voices, creativity, and stories worth documenting.

"I started T-Time because I saw a gap in how transgender men were being represented, documented, and talked about. Too often, our stories are missing from mainstream LGBTQ+ conversations, media coverage, research, and even our own community spaces. I wanted to build something that puts trans men at the center — not just to talk about the challenges we face, but to document our history, amplify our voices, investigate the issues affecting our lives, and create a place where trans men can finally see themselves reflected." — Dominic, on why he started T-Time

Be in it

T-Time runs on other people's stories, not just mine — and that only works if the range is real.

I'm looking for trans men and transmasculine people across race, age, body type, class, sexuality, and background, whether or not you've medically transitioned, whether or not you "pass." If you want to be featured — a portrait, an interview, a story in your own words — start here. Before anything is published, here is what you're agreeing to and what you keep.

  • Written permission first. Nothing runs without it.
  • You set the detail level. Full name, first name, or none.
  • You can withdraw. Before or after publication, no reason needed.
  • No location giveaways. Backgrounds get checked before posting.
  • You see it first. Nothing is published you haven't approved.
  • Adults only. T-Time does not feature minors.

This is a first hello, not a commitment. I'll write back with questions before anything gets made.
